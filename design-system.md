# Portfolio Design System Guidelines

## Aesthetic Vibe
- Style: Retro-Editorial / Warm Creative / High Aesthetic.
- Feel: Tactile, organic, highly crafted, not generic corporate/SaaS.
- Inspiration: a sunset seen from a cliff over the sea. Soft pastel skies and water, with a few vibrant sunset accents.

## Typography
- Headings: Bold Retro Serif or Display Font (e.g., Fraunces or Syne).
- Body: Minimal Monospace or Clean Sans-serif (e.g., Space Mono or Inter).

## Color Palette

### Pastels (large areas)
- Background Primary: Peach Cream (#ffe8d6)
- Card: Butter Yellow (#fbe8a6)
- Card: Sea-Glass Blue (#bfe3e8)
- Card: Sage Green (#cfe0c3)
- Card: Blush Coral (#f9c6b5)

### Vibrant (accents only)
- Main Accent: Sunset Orange (#ff7a2f)
- Accent: Ocean Teal (#118a9a)
- Accent: Fern Green (#2f8f4e)
- Highlight: Sun Yellow (#ffb703)

### Text and dark section
- Deep Ocean Ink (#16303d)

### Hero gradient idea
- Soft gradient from Butter Yellow through Peach Cream and Blush Coral to Sea-Glass Blue, with a Sunset Orange sun shape on top.

### How the colors blend
- Pastels cover about 60% of the page, deep ink and secondary pastels about 30%, vibrant colors only about 10%.
- Each vibrant color sits next to a pastel of its own hue family: orange with blush coral or butter yellow, teal with sea-glass blue, fern with sage.
- Two vibrant colors never touch each other. Put a pastel or ink between them.

### Readability rules
- Deep Ocean Ink text works on every pastel and on orange or yellow.
- Vibrant colors are poor text colors on pastels. Use them as fills (buttons, tags, marquee) with ink text.
- On Ocean Teal and Fern Green fills, use white text, and only for large or bold text.

## Motion & Layout Rules
- Layout: Bento-grid rhythm, multi-column editorial sections, bold marquee banners.
- Animations: Smooth scroll transitions, subtle CSS hover scales, non-cluttered motion.
- Responsiveness: Mobile-first responsive flexbox/grid layout.
