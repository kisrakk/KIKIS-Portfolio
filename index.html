About

Hi, I'm Kisrak.

I'm a web designer and developer at the start of my career, and a freelance photographer on the side.

I'm new to the industry, and I'm upfront about that. What I bring is solid skills, a lot of energy, and the care your project deserves. I build to professional standards, and because I don't have ten years of experience behind me yet, my prices are lower than an established studio's.

I start by listening. I find out what you want and what you need, suggest ways to make it even better, and build on your ideas until the result is what you pictured. Then I make it real.

Photography taught me to notice light, composition and detail, and I bring that eye to every design.
